# Building Your Seed Library (30-50 tracks)

Goal: a small library you know *deeply*, not a big one you don't. For your first
month, 30-50 tracks across three genres is ideal — enough for variety, small
enough that you learn every intro, drop, and outro by heart. That knowledge is
what makes transitions feel intentional instead of lucky.

## Ground rules

1. **Legal only.** Buy, use free-download promos, or Creative Commons. Your
   future gigs depend on a library you can use in public.
2. **Quality floor:** 320kbps MP3 or lossless (FLAC/WAV/AIFF). Never YouTube rips
   — they fall apart on real speakers.
3. **Everything through `_inbox/` first.** Triage weekly: keep only tracks you'd
   actually play. Move keepers into their genre folder; delete the rest.

## Folder + naming convention

```
~/Music/DJ/
├── _inbox/          # new downloads land here, untriaged
├── brasilidades/    # samba, MPB, samba-rock, forró + modern edits
├── funk/            # funk carioca, baile funk, funk BR + edits
└── hiphop/          # boom bap, trap, Brazilian rap
```

Filename: `Artist - Title (Version).ext`
Example: `Jorge Ben - Taj Mahal (Nightmares on Wax Edit).flac`

Keep versions in the name — you *will* end up with 3 edits of the same classic,
and cryptic names like `final_master_v2.wav` are useless at 2 AM on a dark stage.

## Where to dig, per genre

### Brasilidades (90-115 BPM)
| Source | What to search | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Bandcamp](https://bandcamp.com) | tags: `brazilian`, `mpb`, `samba`, `brazil edits`, `organic house brazil` | Best money-to-artist ratio. Weekly "Bandcamp Friday" = 100% to artists. |
| [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com) | "brasilidades edit", "samba edit", "MPB rework", "Jorge Ben edit" | Filter: tracks with a "Free Download" / "Buy" gate. |
| Labels to follow | *Razor-N-Tape*, *Whiskey Disco* (Brazilian disco edits), Brazilian labels like *Goma Gringa*, *Selo Sonar* | Label pages on Bandcamp are gold mines. |

Watch for: live drummers = variable BPM. That's not a flaw — it's your
beatmatching gym. Tag them later; our analyzer (M3) will flag unstable grids.

### Funk BR (125-135 BPM, tamborzão patterns)
| Source | What to search | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SoundCloud | "baile funk edit", "funk carioca", "150 bpm funk" (subgenre), "funk mandelão" | Most funk edit culture lives here. Free DL gates common. |
| Bandcamp | tags: `baile funk`, `funk carioca`, `brazilian funk` | Labels: *Nyege Nyege* adjacent comps, *TORMENTA*, *QTZL*. |
| [Hypeddit](https://hypeddit.com) / label promos | follow funk edit producers | Gated free downloads (follow-to-download). |

Watch for: many funk tracks are short (2-3 min) with abrupt endings — perfect
for practicing quick cuts and echo-out transitions instead of long blends.

### Hiphop (85-95 BPM + trap 130-150 half-time)
| Source | What to search | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bandcamp | tags: `boom bap`, `instrumental hip hop`, `rap nacional` | Brazilian rap (Racionais-era to now) fits your brasilidades bridge. |
| SoundCloud | "hiphop edit", "90s hiphop remix", "rap BR remix" | |
| [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org) | hip-hop section, CC-licensed | Good for practice tracks with zero cost. |

Watch for: the half-time bridge — 90 BPM hiphop mixes into 130+ funk if you
treat the hiphop kick as half-time. This cross-genre trick becomes a signature
move; our crate builder (M5) will surface these pairs automatically.

## Buying checklist (before you pay)

- Preview the *whole* track — does it have a mixable intro/outro (8+ bars of
  reduced arrangement), or does it start with full vocals? Vocal-heavy starts
  need hot-cue tricks; fine, but know before buying.
- Check BPM listed vs. genre expectation. A "funk" edit at 100 BPM might be a
  slowed remix.
- Prefer edits explicitly labeled "DJ edit" / "extended" — someone already added
  mix-friendly intros.

## Weekly rhythm (30 min)

1. Dig: 20 min on one source, one genre. Add to `_inbox/`.
2. Triage: listen at your desk, delete ruthlessly. Keepers → genre folder.
3. Rescan in Mixxx (`Library → Rescan`), let it analyze BPM/key.
4. Play: load two keepers, practice one transition type (see curriculum).

Target: ~10 keepers/week → 40 tracks in a month. Done.
